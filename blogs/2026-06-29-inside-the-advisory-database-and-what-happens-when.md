The GitHub Advisory Database published 1,560 reviewed advisories in May 2026, exceeding historical records, with submissions rising sharply across private reports, repository advisories, and CVE requests. The advisory team recommends submitting complete data, coordinating with maintainers, and requesting CVEs only with clear publication intent.
